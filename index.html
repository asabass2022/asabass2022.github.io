About

About me

I am an urban and built-environment researcher and a Chartered Surveyor. I hold a PhD in Urban and Regional Planning. My training spans land economy, city planning and valuation surveying, which lets me connect spatial policy, land administration and physical site implementation.

My research centres on urban inequality, informality, inclusive urbanism, and urban mobility and transport, across Türkiye and Sub-Saharan Africa. I ask who gets decent housing, safe streets and reliable transport in rapidly urbanising cities, and how planning regulation, infrastructure and socio-spatial design shape those outcomes. I combine large-scale quantitative work (composite indices, census microdata, regression modelling) with qualitative, participatory and psychological methods (policy-elite interviews, Personal Construct Theory and the Repertory Grid Technique).

My work is published in international journals in planning, housing and transport, and is co-authored with researchers on several continents. I keep active academic networks in Türkiye, Europe, the UK, the Gulf, the US, Japan and Africa.

Research

Research programme

Four questions run through my work: how inequality is built into urban space, how informal settlements form and are governed, what makes urbanism inclusive, and how people move through cities.

Urban inequality and informality

Current. Long-running work on Ashaiman, Ghana, from a household survey on slum regeneration to a multidimensional composite index of settlement trajectories, and national-scale measurement of socio-spatial inequality in housing quality from Ghana's 2021 census microdata, released with an open dataset.

Next. Extending the housing-quality methodology to Turkish cities, to show where deprivation concentrates and how regeneration and upgrading policies reach, or miss, the people who need them.

Inclusive urbanism: housing, health and the built environment

Current. A four-dimensional framework for housing deficits in Greater Accra within SDG 11, a systematic synthesis of the built environment's role in healthy living, and a manuscript in preparation on perceived earthquake risk and housing vulnerability in Türkiye.

Next. Repertory Grid and Personal Construct Theory studies of how residents perceive the aesthetics, comfort and safety of everyday streetscapes, and GIS work that overlays seismic hazard, urban heat and building stock to show where social vulnerability and physical hazard coincide.

Urban mobility and transport

Current. A linked series of manuscripts on jurisdictional fragmentation, paratransit governance and public transport mode preferences in Greater Accra, plus published work on paratransit integration and on seating equity for disadvantaged riders in Ankara.

Next. A comparative study of multimodal integration, pedestrian connectivity and mode choice in Turkish metropolitan areas, using stated and revealed preference modelling alongside streetscape audits and appraisal of low-emission first- and last-mile interventions.

Methods and tools

Survey design and mixed-methods research; multivariate statistics, including ordinal logistic regression and composite indices (SPSS, R); thematic analysis and elite interviewing (NVivo); Repertory Grid Technique and Personal Construct Theory; systematic and scoping reviews; GIS (ArcGIS, working knowledge); AutoCAD (basic).

Teaching

Teaching interests

  • Housing Policy and Housing Economics. Housing systems, affordability, finance and speculation, informality, and the design and evaluation of housing programmes.
  • Transport Policy and Urban Mobility. Public transport and paratransit governance, mode choice, and equity in access to mobility.
  • Urban Economics, Land Economics and Land Administration. Land and housing markets, valuation, tenure, zoning, development appraisal and real estate feasibility.
  • History and Theory of the Built Environment. Evolution of urban form, public space, housing typologies, socio-spatial justice and comparative urbanisation across Türkiye and the Global South.
  • Ecology, Climate and Environmental Design. Climate-adaptive neighbourhoods, urban heat and seismic resilience, healthy built environments, green infrastructure and SDG 11.
  • Urban Design Studio, Site Analysis and Research Methods. Evidence-based site diagnostics, GIS, participatory user-perception methods, survey design, index construction and multi-criteria decision analysis.
